Title:
Tamagotchi Murdoku 12x12: Quiet and social

Intro:
Eleven friends and the devil Akumatchi fill Tamagotchi Town.
Place every figure on a free square, one per row and one per column. Squares with a blocking object cannot be used; figures may stand on standable objects.
Quiet and social squares have special rules; unmarked squares have neither restriction.
