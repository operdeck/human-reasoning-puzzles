Tamagotchi Murdoku 9x9: The devil by the stream

Intro:
A stream splits Tamagotchi Town in two. Water squares are blocked, but the three bridge squares can be occupied. The coloured regions are the areas; people on unmarked squares can also share an area.
Eight friends and the devil Akumatchi are out and about.
Place every figure on a free square, one per row and one per column. Squares with a blocking object cannot be used; standable objects can.
