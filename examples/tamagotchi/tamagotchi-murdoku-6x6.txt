# Tamagotchi Murdoku - "Where is the devil?"  (6x6)
#
# Original puzzle made for this repo. Five friends and the devil Akumatchi wander
# Tamagotchi Town. Akumatchi is placed last and is alone with one friend: who?

Size: 6

Regions:
a: garden #CBE6B9
b: school #FADFB5

e: arcade #F3EDA2

a a a b b b
a a a b b b
a a c c b b
d d c c e e
d d d e e e
d d d e e e

Objects:
sakura: blocking r1c1
fountain: blocking r3c3
arcade: blocking r5c5
chair: standable r2c5
carpet: standable r5c1 r5c2

People:
Mametchi

Lovelitchi
Masktchi
Akumatchi

Attributes:
friend: Mametchi Kuchipatchi Memetchi Lovelitchi Masktchi

# The question - where is the devil? - is not a clue. Akumatchi has no clues of
# his own except that he ends up alone with exactly one friend; the rest pin him down.
Words:
target.attribute: a {name}

Clues:
next_to Masktchi sakura
adjacent_regions Kuchipatchi Masktchi
in_corner Memetchi
adjacent_regions Kuchipatchi Lovelitchi
left_of Memetchi Lovelitchi
not_next_to Kuchipatchi fountain
apart Memetchi Lovelitchi
only_on Mametchi chair
