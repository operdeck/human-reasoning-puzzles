Title:
Tamagotchi Murdoku 6x6: A quiet spot

Intro:
Five friends and Akumatchi wander through Tamagotchi Town, looking for places to spend the day.
The coloured regions are the areas; someone on an unmarked square may also be alone.
Place every figure on a free square, one per row and one per column. Squares with a blocking object cannot be used; standable objects can.
