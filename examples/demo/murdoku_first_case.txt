# Murdoku - "The first case"  (4x4, three regions)
#
# A first, tiny Murdoku to show how the puzzle works.
#
# Three suspects and one victim, one per row and column. Ingrid, Joost and
# Lotte each have a clue; Wouter, the victim, takes the last free square.
# Wouter was alone with his murderer: the one other person in his region
# did it.
#
# Solution: Joost r1c1, Wouter r2c3, Ingrid r3c4, Lotte r4c2, so Ingrid is
# the murderer (she is the only other person in the sitting room).

Size: 4
Language: en

Words:
patio: on the patio

Regions:
p: patio #CBE6B9
g: guest_room #F4CBC3
s: sitting_room #FADFB5

Grid:
p p p p
p g s s
g g s s
g g s s

Objects:
tree: blocking r1c2
bush: blocking r1c3
table: blocking r2c4
cabinet: blocking r4c4

# Chairs and the bed are standable; the bed is 1x2.
chair: standable r3c1
chair: standable r3c4
chair: standable r4c3
bed: standable r4c1 r4c2

People:
Ingrid
Joost
Lotte
Wouter

Clues:
on Ingrid chair
next_to Joost tree
on Lotte bed

# Booklet text for --docx (optional).
Title:
Murdoku: The first case

Intro:
Three suspects and a victim stand in a small house. Place everyone on a free square, one per row and one per column.
Objects block their square, except a chair or a bed, which people can use.
Wouter, the victim, is placed last and was alone with his murderer.

Question:
Who is the only other person in Wouter's room: who did it?

Reveal:
Ingrid did it: she shares the sitting room with Wouter.
