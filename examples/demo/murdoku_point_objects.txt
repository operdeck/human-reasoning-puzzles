Title:
Murdoku: Mice in the garden

Intro:
Two mice, a fountain, a lantern and some cheese sit on the grid lines of this garden, not inside the squares.
Whoever stands in a square touching such a point is next to it, even when that square lies in another area.

Question:
Where does everyone stand?
